01The problem

Drowsy driving is quiet. There's no warning shot — just a longer blink, then a longer one.

→ → → →

Objective

  • Detect early signs of drowsiness from eyes, face and head.
  • Monitor the driver continuously through a camera.
  • Alert early, on prolonged eye closure or combined signs.
  • Reduce risk by making the driver aware before control is affected.

Our solution

A camera watches the driver's face. Eye closure, blinking, yawning and head movement are measured every frame. If the signs persist, the system flags a possible drowsy state and plays an audio alert.

05Detection & decision logic

One signal can lie. A blink is not sleep. So the system combines several signs over time.

Eye closure

Is the eye closed longer than a normal blink? Measured with the Eye Aspect Ratio.

Blink analysis

Short closures are treated as normal blinks — no false alarms.

Yawning

Mouth opening is tracked with a Mouth Aspect Ratio.

Head movement

Turn and tilt of the head is an extra sign of reduced attention.

Combined detection

Signs are weighed together, so the call is more reliable than any single signal.

The core formula

EAR = ( ‖p2 − p6‖ + ‖p3 − p5‖ ) / ( 2 · ‖p1 − p4‖ )

p1…p6 are six landmarks around one eye. Open eye ≈ 0.3, closed eye → near 0.

NormalBlinks and normal movement. No action.
WarningProlonged closure, or a yawn / head sign persisting.
DrowsyEnough combined evidence. Audio alert triggers.

Advantages

  • Continuous real-time monitoring
  • Early warning
  • Simple setup, no extra hardware
  • Easy to use

Limitations

  • Low light reduces accuracy
  • Camera must see the face
  • Sunglasses / face covering hide the eyes
  • Blink patterns differ between people

DriveGuard AI is an assistance tool. It does not replace driver attention or safe driving.
